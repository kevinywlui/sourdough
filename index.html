How is this calculated?

Hydration is total water ÷ total flour, counting what's inside your starter too — a 100%-hydration starter is half flour, half water.

Each flour absorbs a different amount of water, so each gets its own target: all-purpose 67%, bread flour 72%, whole wheat 80%. Whole wheat runs highest because its bran and germ soak up extra water; bread flour's higher protein takes a bit more than all-purpose.

The number above is the average of those targets, weighted by how much of each flour ends up in the dough — the flours you add plus the starter's own flour. Shift the blend toward whole wheat and it rises; toward all-purpose and it falls. The − / + buttons nudge it from there, and ↺ returns to the blend's default.
